# CS-KMITL Services Portal (`DESIGN.md`)

Inherits the root [CS-KMITL Design System](../DESIGN.md).

## Direction

- **Product**: Central service directory and Passion Group hub for CS-KMITL students and faculty.
- **Character**: `calm, structured, utilitarian` — quick to scan, zero visual noise.
- **Moments**: The service directory grid with live status dots and category filtering.
- **Signature**: Hairline 1px cards, `tabular-nums` service count, and monospace service domain/type tags.
